The Operator

Armen Nalband

Nalbana Properties is a privately held portfolio of luxury short-term and long-term rentals in Miami, FL and Yucca Valley, CA — built, designed, and self-managed by Armen Nalband since 2021.

Every property was acquired with a hands-on renovation and a clear design vision: create homes that feel like boutique hotels, not vacation rentals. The result is a portfolio that commands premium nightly rates, attracts repeat guests, and maintains five-star ratings across platforms.

With backgrounds in finance and operations, Armen manages every aspect of the business in-house — from pricing strategy and revenue management to guest experience and property maintenance.
