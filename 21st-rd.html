Layout

Floor Plan

Villa Nalbana is a refined Brickell-area retreat designed around a private pool courtyard. The single-story home maximizes indoor-outdoor living, with the main living and dining areas opening directly onto the pool terrace through wide glass doors.

Four bedrooms are arranged for privacy — the master suite occupies its own wing with a luxurious en-suite bath, while three guest bedrooms share two additional bathrooms. The fully-equipped kitchen and open dining room anchor the social heart of the home.
