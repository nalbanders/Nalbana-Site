Floor Plan

Villa Marqueza is a sprawling single-story residence centered on a lush private pool and bougainvillea-draped courtyard. The home's generous footprint accommodates five bedrooms arranged across two wings, ideal for multi-family stays or group gatherings.

The main living wing features an open-plan kitchen, dining, and living room with access to the pool terrace. The bedroom wing separates the master suite from four additional bedrooms, each with curated furnishings and generous closets.
