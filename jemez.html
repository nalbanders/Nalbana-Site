Four Winds Trail is a classic desert hideaway nestled in the heart of Yucca Valley, steps from Joshua Tree National Park. The low-profile single-story home sits on a generous desert lot, embracing the stark beauty of its surroundings through panoramic windows and an expansive outdoor deck.

A serene living room with mountain views, a fully equipped kitchen, and three well-appointed bedrooms make it a perfect base for stargazing, hiking, and deep desert rest. The outdoor lounge area with fire pit is where the real magic happens — especially after sundown.

Set in the sweeping high desert of Yucca Valley, Four Winds Trail sits at the gateway to Joshua Tree National Park. The property is surrounded by iconic boulder formations and ancient Joshua Trees — a landscape that transforms hourly as the desert light shifts from dawn to the most breathtaking night skies you've ever seen.
