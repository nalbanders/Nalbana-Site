Floor Plan

Villa Maganda is a single-story open-plan home centered around an indoor-outdoor connection. The living and dining areas flow seamlessly through floor-to-ceiling sliders to the pool deck and tropical garden.

The bedroom wing sits privately off the main living space, anchored by a unique master suite with a loft and exposed wood beams. A second bedroom with twin beds and a third bedroom round out the layout.
