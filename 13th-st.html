Buko House is a beautifully restored Little Havana home with warm, intimate character. Named for the Filipino term of endearment, the property reflects the rich multicultural tapestry of its neighborhood — artfully designed, personally curated, and full of soul.

Three bedrooms, a fully updated kitchen, and a private outdoor garden make it ideal for couples, small families, or friend groups seeking a genuine Miami neighborhood experience a few minutes from both Brickell and Wynwood.
