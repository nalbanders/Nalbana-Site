Floor Plan

Villa Armaza is defined by its dramatic poolside colonnade — a signature architectural element that creates a resort-like atmosphere in the heart of Brickell. The home wraps around an expansive pool courtyard with colonnaded loggias on multiple sides.

Inside, four generous bedrooms each with en-suite bathrooms offer complete privacy for groups. The open living, dining, and kitchen areas flow seamlessly onto the pool terrace, blurring the line between interior and exterior.
